## Video direction

Seven directed scenes on a 144 BPM grid, rendered at 24 fps. Near-black canvas, ivory type, one mint accent. Barlow Condensed 900 headlines, JetBrains Mono labels. A single ECG motif ties the reveal to the closing. Hero, naming and final card deliberately pause for a read; the working scenes keep revealing actions across their duration. Graphic objects depict mechanisms, not a fake dashboard. Caption track disabled. All files and messages shown are synthetic illustrations.
